Pofu vs Lifesum: which calorie counter fits you?

By Ali Gürkan Sevilmiş, developer of Pofu · Published November 6, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026

A fair comparison of Pofu and Lifesum for people who want to log food quickly and stay consistent. I build Pofu, so I say where Lifesum is stronger.

The short answer

According to its App Store listing on November 6, 2026, Lifesum lets you log by photo, voice, text or barcode, and adds meal plans, recipes, a weekly Life Score and Apple Health sync. It is free to download with limited features, and Premium is sold as 1-month, 3-month and annual subscriptions. It also has a Google Play listing.

Pofu is Apple-only (iPhone and iPad) and narrower, but adds optional cycle awareness, grace days and a visible confidence score on photo scans. If you want a broader wellness app on more than one platform, Lifesum is the stronger fit.

Pricing and features change. Competitor details come from Lifesum's App Store listing and website as read on November 6, 2026; check them for what it offers today. If a detail is not listed here, I could not verify it.

Who each is better for

Choose Lifesum if…

  • You want one app for calories, water, recipes and meal plans.
  • You use Android or want a Google Play version.
  • You like the idea of a weekly score for the whole day, not only calories.
  • You want many ways to log, including voice and text.

Choose Pofu if…

  • You want a visible confidence score on each AI photo scan.
  • You want optional cycle awareness with data kept on your device.
  • You want a streak protected by grace days instead of broken by one off day.
  • You want an Apple Watch app and Live Activities for logging and fasting.

Neither choice is wrong. The right calorie counter is the one you will still open in week three, and both can be tried before you pay.

Questions

Is Pofu better than Lifesum?

It depends on what you value. Lifesum has meal plans, recipes, more logging methods and far more App Store ratings. Pofu is built around women's routines on iPhone, with optional cycle awareness, grace days and AI photo scans that show a confidence score. Try both.

Does Lifesum track your cycle?

According to its App Store listing on November 6, 2026, cycle tracking is not mentioned. Pofu has optional cycle awareness, off by default, with data stored on your device. Pofu does not predict periods or ovulation.

Is Lifesum free?

The listing says Lifesum is free to download and use with limited features, with Premium subscriptions for the full experience. Pofu is also free to start, and Pofu Pro costs $9.99/month, $49.99/year with a 7-day free trial, or $199.99 lifetime (US).

Does Pofu work on Android?

Not right now. Pofu is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch app. Lifesum has a Google Play listing.
