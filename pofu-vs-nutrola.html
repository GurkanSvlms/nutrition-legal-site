Pofu vs Nutrola: which calorie counter fits you?

By Ali Gürkan Sevilmiş, developer of Pofu · Published November 6, 2026 · Updated November 6, 2026

A fair comparison of Pofu and Nutrola for people who want to log food quickly and stay consistent. I build Pofu, so I say where Nutrola is stronger.

The short answer

According to its website and App Store listing on November 6, 2026, Nutrola is an AI calorie counter with photo and voice logging, barcode scanning, recipes, fasting, Apple Health and Apple Watch sync, on iOS and Android. It says its whole-food values come from USDA FoodData Central.

Both apps lean on AI photo logging. Nutrola mentions no cycle features, while Pofu adds optional cycle awareness, grace days and a visible confidence score on photo scans. Nutrola is on Android; Pofu is not.

Pricing and features change. Competitor details come from Nutrola's App Store listing and website as read on November 6, 2026; check them for what it offers today. If a detail is not listed here, I could not verify it.

Who each is better for

Choose Nutrola if…

  • You use Android or want a Google Play version.
  • You want voice logging and a recipe library next to photo logging.
  • You like the USDA-based database approach for whole foods.
  • You want a custom fasting window such as 20:4.

Choose Pofu if…

  • You want free, unlimited barcode, search and manual logging.
  • You want optional cycle awareness that explains a luteal-phase scale jump.
  • You want grace days that protect a streak.
  • You want a visible confidence score on each AI photo scan, with corrections remembered.

Neither choice is wrong. The right calorie counter is the one you will still open in week three, and both can be tried before you pay.

Questions

Is Pofu better than Nutrola?

It depends on what you value. Nutrola is on Android and has a recipe library and a custom fasting window. Pofu is Apple-only (iPhone and iPad) but adds optional cycle awareness, grace days and a confidence score on photo scans. Both are young apps with few ratings, so try both.

Does Nutrola have cycle features?

According to its website and App Store listing on November 6, 2026, cycle features are not mentioned. Pofu offers optional cycle awareness, off by default, with data on your device. It does not predict periods or ovulation.

Which has free AI photo scans?

Pofu includes 3 free AI photo scans on day one and 1 a day after, plus 1 free voice log a day. Nutrola's listing marks AI meal scan as a premium feature that needs a subscription, so check the App Store for its current offer.

Does Pofu work on Android?

Not right now. Pofu is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch app. Nutrola's website links to Google Play.
