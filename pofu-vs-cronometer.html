The short answer

According to its website and App Store listing on November 6, 2026, Cronometer is built for detailed nutrition: a curated database of 1.1 million+ verified foods, 95+ vitamins and minerals, and sync with Apple Health, Fitbit, Garmin, Oura and Whoop. It runs on web, iOS and Android.

Pofu does not track micronutrients and is Apple-only (iPhone and iPad). If you care about vitamins, minerals and database depth, Cronometer is the stronger tool. Pofu is lighter, with optional cycle awareness, grace days and AI photo scans with a visible confidence score.

Pricing and features change. Competitor details come from Cronometer's App Store listing and website as read on November 6, 2026; check them for what it offers today. If a detail is not listed here, I could not verify it.

Who each is better for

Choose Cronometer if…

  • You want vitamins, minerals and other micronutrients tracked.
  • You want a curated database and a web app as well as a phone app.
  • You use Android or a mix of devices.
  • You want to import data from Fitbit, Garmin, Oura or Whoop.

Choose Pofu if…

  • You want to log a meal from a photo and see how confident the AI is.
  • You want optional cycle awareness that explains a luteal-phase scale jump.
  • You want something lighter than a full nutrient report.
  • You want grace days, widgets, Live Activities and an Apple Watch app.

Neither choice is wrong. The right calorie counter is the one you will still open in week three, and both can be tried before you pay.

Questions

Is Pofu better than Cronometer?

No single answer. Cronometer is stronger on nutrient detail, database curation, web access and Android. Pofu is lighter and adds optional cycle awareness, grace days and photo scans with a visible confidence score. Both can be tried for free.

Does Cronometer track your cycle?

According to its website and App Store listing on November 6, 2026, cycle tracking is not mentioned; the site only refers to biometrics in general. Pofu has optional cycle awareness, off by default, with data kept on your device. Pofu does not predict periods or ovulation.

Is Cronometer free?

Its website and listing describe a free version and a paid Gold tier, and say barcode scanning is free. The listing places photo and voice logging and the fasting tracker in Gold. Pofu is free to start with unlimited barcode, search and manual logging, 3 free AI photo scans on day one and 1 a day after.

Does Pofu work on Android or the web?

Not right now. Pofu is an iPhone app with an Apple Watch app. Cronometer is available on web, iOS and Android according to its website.
