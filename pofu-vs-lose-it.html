Pofu vs Lose It!: which calorie counter fits you?

By Ali Gürkan Sevilmiş, developer of Pofu · Published November 2, 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026

A fair, general comparison of Pofu and Lose It! for people who want to log food quickly and stay consistent.

The short version

Lose It! is a well-known calorie counter oriented toward weight loss goals, with food logging and barcode scanning. Pofu is an AI calorie counter made for women on iPhone, with photo logging, optional cycle awareness, grace days and fasting windows.

Both help you stay inside a calorie target. The difference is how they talk to you on a hard week, and which extras sit beside the log.

Pricing and features change. This page describes Lose It! only in general terms, so check its App Store listing for what it offers today.

Who each is better for

Lose It! may suit you if

  • You want a tracker framed around a weight-loss plan.
  • You like its logging flow and food search after trying it.
  • You are comfortable with its approach to goals and feedback.

Pofu may suit you if

  • You want goals from a 2-minute quiz covering weight loss, maintenance or simply healthy eating.
  • You want the scale to be read with your cycle in mind, if you opt in.
  • You want shorter fasting windows to count as a win.
  • You want a widget and Apple Watch app that log in two taps.

Neither choice is wrong. The best calorie counter is the one you will still open in week three, and many apps offer a free way to try them.

Questions

Is Pofu better than Lose It!?

It depends on what you value. Both can help you stay within a calorie target. Pofu is built around women's routines: optional cycle awareness, grace days, gentle fasting windows and AI photo logging. Lose It! may suit you better if its approach matches how you like to track. Try both, since both can be started without paying up front, and check each App Store listing for current details.

Is Pofu free compared with Lose It!?

Pofu is free to start. Barcode scanning, search and manual logging are free and unlimited, and you get 3 free AI photo scans on your first day and 1 every day after that. Pofu Pro is optional. Lose It! has its own free and paid options that change over time, so check its App Store listing.

Does Pofu work on Android?

Not right now. Pofu is an iOS app for iPhone and iPad, with an Apple Watch app. You can get it on the App Store.

Is Pofu only for weight loss?

No. Pofu's quiz builds goals for weight loss, maintenance or simply healthy eating. It is not a medical tool, and anyone can use it.
