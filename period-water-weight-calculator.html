Period water weight calculator: is it water or fat?

The scale jumped before or during your period? Enter three numbers and see whether the change looks like typical cycle-related water retention.

How this calculator works

  • Where you are in your cycle: it counts days from the start of your last period and uses your usual cycle length. Days 1–5 are treated as your period, and the last week of the cycle as the days before your period. It never predicts your next period.
  • The water window: water retention is most commonly reported in the days before and the first days of a period. In that window, a rise of up to about 5 lb (2.3 kg) is labelled “most likely water”.
  • Outside that window: changes up to about 1 kg (2.2 lb) are treated as everyday fluctuation, and larger ones as hard to explain with your cycle.

These cut-offs are simple rules based on the sources below, not a diagnosis. Bodies differ, and some people see no change at all.

What the research says

  • In a study of 42 women, body weight was on average about 0.45 kg higher during menstruation than in the first week of the cycle, and the change tracked water, not fat.
  • Health sources such as Healthline describe typical period-related gains of a few pounds, mostly water, that go away within days. Cleveland Clinic suggests seeing a doctor if your weight changes more than 5 pounds across your cycle.
  • The NHS lists bloating among common premenstrual symptoms.

Frequently asked questions

How much weight is normal to gain on your period?

Many sources describe about 1 to 5 pounds (0.5 to 2.3 kg), mostly water, and some people gain nothing. A study of 42 women found an average of about 0.45 kg during menstruation.

When does period water weight go away?

Usually within a few days after your period starts. Weigh yourself again about a week after your period starts, at the same time of day, to see your usual weight.

Do calories eaten on your period count?

Yes. Resting energy use may rise slightly in the luteal phase, but research suggests the difference is small, so calories still count.

Should I eat less when the scale jumps before my period?

Not because of one reading. Water weight is temporary, so wait for the trend after your period before changing your plan.

Does this tool store my data?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing you enter is sent or saved.
