When does period water weight go away?

For many people it eases within a few days after the period starts and is usually gone by the end of it. The exact timing varies from person to person and cycle to cycle.

What to expect

Fluid-related weight tends to peak just before or at the start of bleeding and then drift down. In one study the average was about 0.45 kg higher during menstruation, which is small enough to disappear into normal daily fluctuation once it settles.

What helps while you wait

  • Drink water normally; cutting fluids does not help and can backfire.
  • Keep eating your usual meals instead of skipping food to force the number down.
  • Light movement and good sleep often make bloating feel better.
  • Weigh at the same time of day, and compare against the same cycle day last month.

When it is not just water

If the extra weight is still there after a full cycle, or keeps building over several cycles, look at your weekly trend and consider talking to a doctor. Cleveland Clinic advises seeing one if the change across a cycle is more than about 5 lb.
