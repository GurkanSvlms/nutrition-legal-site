What should I do after missing a day of calorie tracking?

Just log your next meal as usual. Do not try to make up for the missed day by eating less or by logging everything from memory, because one gap changes very little.

Why it matters little

Weight change comes from weeks of intake, not one day. Research on self-monitoring links regular logging with better weight loss (the evidence is modest), and that comes from the overall habit, not a perfect record.

What to do

  • Restart at the next meal, not "tomorrow morning".
  • If you remember it roughly, log a quick estimate and move on; a rough number beats an empty day.
  • Do not skip meals to "pay back" the day. That tends to lead to rebound hunger.
  • Make logging easier: use photos, barcodes or saved meals.

Build in slack

Pofu is our app. It gives up to 2 automatic grace days a month, so a day you cannot log is allowed for. Whatever tool you use, plan for imperfect days; consistency over months is what shows in the results. Get Pofu on the App Store.
