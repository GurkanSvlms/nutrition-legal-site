How much weight do you gain on your period?

Most people gain about 1 to 5 lb (0.5 to 2.3 kg) around their period, and it is mostly water rather than fat. In one study the average was about 0.45 kg higher during menstruation, and some people see no change at all.

Why the scale moves

Hormone changes around your period can make your body hold more fluid and cause bloating. The NHS lists bloating among common premenstrual symptoms. Because it is fluid, it usually fades within days instead of building up like fat.

How to read it

  • Compare the same cycle days across months, for example day 3 this month with day 3 last month.
  • Use a weekly average instead of single mornings.
  • Do not cut food because of one high reading; wait for the trend.

When to see a doctor

Cleveland Clinic suggests talking to a doctor if your weight changes by more than about 5 lb across a cycle, or if the gain does not settle after your period. Irregular cycles, severe pain or sudden swelling are also worth a check.
