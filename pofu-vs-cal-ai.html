Pofu vs Cal AI: which calorie counter fits you?

By Ali Gürkan Sevilmiş, developer of Pofu · Published November 2, 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026

A fair, general comparison of Pofu and Cal AI for people who want to log food quickly and stay consistent.

The short version

Cal AI and Pofu both let you log a meal by photographing it, so the real question is what you want around the photo. Cal AI is widely known as a calorie counter built around photo logging. Pofu is an AI calorie counter made for women that pairs photo scanning with optional cycle awareness, grace days, fasting windows and macro tracking.

If you only want a quick snap-and-log experience, either app may do the job. If you want an app that adapts its tone to your cycle and does not punish missed days, Pofu was designed for that.

Pricing and features change. This page describes Cal AI only in general terms, based on its public App Store listing as viewed on November 2, 2026. Check the listing for what it offers today.

Who each is better for

Cal AI may suit you if

  • You mainly want fast photo logging and a minimal interface.
  • You do not need cycle-aware feedback or fasting tools in the same app.
  • You prefer its look and feel after trying it.

Pofu may suit you if

  • You want feedback that treats a pre-period scale jump as possibly water, not failure.
  • You want grace days and a weekly balance instead of broken streaks.
  • You also fast, and want a 14:10 or 16:8 window and your meal log in one app.
  • You want free barcode, search and manual logging, with a few AI photo scans to start.

Neither choice is wrong. The best calorie counter is the one you will still open in week three, and many apps offer a free way to try them.

Questions

Does Pofu work on Android?

Not right now. Pofu is an iOS app for iPhone and iPad, with an Apple Watch app. You can get it on the App Store.

Is Pofu better than Cal AI?

It depends on what you value. If photo logging is all you need, both apps can do it. Pofu is built around women's routines: optional cycle awareness, grace days, gentle fasting windows and AI photo logging. Cal AI may suit you better if its approach matches how you like to track. Try Pofu free, and check Cal AI's App Store listing for what is free before you start, since free use there is limited and details change.

Is Pofu free compared with Cal AI?

Pofu is free to start. Barcode scanning, search and manual logging are free and unlimited, and you get 3 free AI photo scans on your first day and 1 every day after that. Pofu Pro is optional. Cal AI has a paid tier and free use is limited; its options change over time, so check its App Store listing.
