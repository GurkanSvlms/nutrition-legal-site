FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

Something else? Write to support.

Is Pofu only for women?

No — anyone can use it. Pofu is designed with women in mind: an optional cycle-aware tone, grace days for missed days and language that never shames you. If cycle tracking isn’t relevant to you, just leave it off.

Do I have to share my cycle data?

No. Cycle awareness is opt-in, and you can turn it off or delete the date anytime. If you use it, your cycle data stays on your iPhone. It isn’t medical advice and it doesn’t predict or diagnose anything.

Is there an Android version?

Not right now. Pofu is made for iPhone, with an Apple Watch app and home screen widgets.

How accurate is the AI?

Photo estimates are a smart starting point, not a lab measurement — portion size and hidden oil are hard to judge from a picture. That’s why every entry is editable before you save it, and when you correct a portion Pofu remembers it for next time. For packaged food, scan the barcode.

What’s free, and what’s in Pro?

Free: manual meals, water and daily macros, cycle awareness, grace days, your first AI photo scan and one AI voice log every day. Pro adds AI photo and voice logging for every meal, the AI coach, a flexible weekend plan, fasting statistics and color themes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple: cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions at least 24 hours before renewal, and you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel.
